# Intelligent Customer Analytics Platform

## Business Problem

Businesses collect large amounts of customer data but often struggle to convert it into actionable insights.

Without proper analysis, companies cannot:
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Identify high-value customers
- Detect customers likely to churn
- Design effective marketing campaigns
- Improve customer retention

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to build an end-to-end Intelligent Customer Analytics Platform that transforms raw customer data into business insights using data analysis, machine learning, and interactive dashboards.

## Business Goals

- Understand customer demographics
- Analyze purchasing behavior
- Identify important customer segments
- Predict customer churn
- Recommend targeted marketing strategies

## Expected Outcome

The platform should help business teams make data-driven decisions that improve customer satisfaction and increase revenue.

## Success Metrics

- Accurate customer segmentation
- High-performing churn prediction model
- Interactive dashboard for business users
- Clear business recommendations
